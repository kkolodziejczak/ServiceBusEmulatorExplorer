Offline Mermaid Viewer

Use this shared viewer from the spec root. Specs can pass their embedded Mermaid diagrams through the URL hash.

Drag a diagram to pan. Hold Ctrl and scroll over a diagram to zoom that chart. Double-click a diagram to center it.

Example Flow

flowchart TD
  A[Start from exported diagram] --> B[Extract workflow intent]
  B --> C{Enough detail?}
  C -->|Yes| D[Draft skill instructions]
  C -->|No| E[Ask targeted clarification]
  E --> D
  D --> F[Validate skill behavior]
