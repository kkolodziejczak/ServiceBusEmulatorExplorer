# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Service Bus Emulator Explorer to be safe, respectful, and productive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar project context.
- Respect privacy and remove sensitive data from examples, logs, and screenshots.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while discussing them constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, customer data, private message content, or security details in public channels.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary disagreement, first try to clarify the technical concern respectfully. For harassment, threats, privacy concerns, or other sensitive conduct issues, contact the maintainer privately using the contact options on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/kkolodziejczak). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical, and conflicts of interest should be disclosed.
